Easy branching

Use two spaces before a child. Optional codes such as A:, B1: and B2: make editing easier; the codes are hidden in the chart. Add an explanation to a node with | note text on the next line.

To override the line origin for one item, add [from=word] at the end of its line. You can also define codes and draw explicit links, for example A ->|YES| B1. Use **bold**, *italic*, __underline__, and /n within any item or note.
